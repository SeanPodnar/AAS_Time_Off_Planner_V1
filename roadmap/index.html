AAS Time Off Planner: roadmap and backlog

Add ideas to the backlog, rank them, and promote them into a release when they are ready. Lower rank number means higher priority within a stage.

Stages run Backlog, then Later (v1.2), Next (v1.1), Now (v1), then Done. Promote moves an item one stage toward Now and places it last in that stage. Blocked items wait on something outside our control.
